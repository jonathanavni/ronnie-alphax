# Running feedback sessions with older adults

The aim is to learn whether the **idea and direction** work for older travellers. Polishing the screens comes later.

## Before

- **Recruit** 5–8 people aged 65+ through grandparents, family friends, a community centre or a club. Include **at least one person over 80** if you can. Ask one family member who helps with travel to join a separate short session.
- **Device:** if possible, open the prototype link on **their own phone**, with their usual text size. Bring a charged phone as a backup.
- **Print task cards**, one task per card, in large type.
- Plan **30–45 minutes**. Have water ready. Remind them to bring their glasses.

## Opening (say this)

> "Thank you for helping. I'm testing **the app, not you**. There are no wrong answers, and if something is confusing, that's the app's fault and exactly what I need to know. I'm not selling anything. Nothing here is real — no bookings, no calls. You can stop or take a break any time."

## Warm-up questions (5 min)

1. Tell me about the last trip you took. What was hard about it?
2. Who helps you plan trips? How?
3. What worries you most when you travel?

## Tasks (20 min). Watch, don't help

Give them one card at a time and let them try. If they ask "What do I do?", answer: "What would you try?" Help only after they've been stuck for about a minute.

| # | Task card | What you're learning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Have a look at the app. What do you think it's for?" | Is the idea clear right away? |
| 2 | "Plan a short holiday somewhere warm." | Does the chat feel easy? Do they tap the replies or type? |
| 3 | "Find out what you're doing on Tuesday." | Can they find "My trip"? Do the accessibility notes help? |
| 4 | "It's Tuesday morning. How do you get to your first stop?" | Does in-trip help ("Today") make sense? |
| 5 | "You feel unwell and want to reach your daughter." | Is Help easy to find and calming? |
| 6 | "Make the writing bigger." | Can they find the setting? |

If they get tired of thinking out loud, let them finish the task quietly and then ask: "Tell me what you did there."

## Closing questions (10 min)

1. What did you like most? What would you change?
2. Would you trust an assistant like this to plan your trip? Why or why not?
3. How do you feel about your family seeing your plan or where you are?
4. Would you use this? Would you pay for it? *(Optional)*
5. On a scale of 1–5: how easy was it? How useful would it be?

## Take notes on

- Where they **hesitated, mis-tapped or got lost** (screen and button)
- Exact **quotes**, the best material for the school presentation
- Anything they **tried to tap that isn't tappable**
- Whether they used **Read aloud**, the **mic**, or **typing**

Record each session in `docs/feedback-log.md`: date, person (first name or initials, age range), observations, quotes, top 3 issues. After 3 sessions, look for patterns and update the plan.
