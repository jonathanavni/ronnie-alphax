2. `npm run preview` and click through every flow from `#/demo` at **375px** and **320px** wide, and once at the **largest text size**. No console errors, no sideways scrolling, nothing cut off.
3. Run the **screen checklist** in `docs/ux-guidelines.md` on every changed screen.
4. Re-read all new wording as a 75‑year‑old would: plain, short, respectful, no jargon.
5. Report: what was checked, what passed, anything left out.
